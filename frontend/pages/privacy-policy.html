Privacy Protocol & Data Security

At Maison & Co., client confidentiality and data restraint are core tenets of our philosophy. We collect only the information required to process and dispatch acquisitions safely to your designated address.

1. Information Collected

When you register an account or place an order, we retain:

  • Your full name and contact telephone number (for courier rider communication).
  • Physical delivery address details.
  • Order history, items acquired, and applied privilege coupons.

2. Payment Security & Absence of Card Storage

Because Maison & Co. operates strictly via Cash on Delivery (COD), we never process, collect, or store sensitive credit card numbers, debit cards, bank credentials, or CVV codes. Payment occurs physically between you and the courier representative upon delivery.

3. Courier Logistics Sharing

Your delivery name, contact number, and address are transmitted exclusively to our contracted courier dispatch services (TCS, Leopards, Call Courier) solely for package transit and cash collection.

4. Client Rights & Rectification

You may modify your personal profile or purge your saved shipping addresses at any time directly through your Customer Account Dashboard.
