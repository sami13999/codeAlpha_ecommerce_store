Terms & Conditions of Acquisition

These Terms govern all catalog reservations, parcel orders, and customer relations with Maison & Co.. By placing an acquisition order on this platform, you affirm your acceptance of these operational protocols.

1. Cash on Delivery (COD) Obligations

All retail acquisitions made through this application are processed exclusively under Cash on Delivery terms. The purchaser agrees to have the exact cash amount in Pakistani Rupees (PKR) available at the designated delivery address when the courier representative arrives.

2. Order Cancellation Protocol

Customers may cancel any order directly through their account dashboard while the order remains in PENDING status. Once cancelled, reserved product inventory is automatically returned to the public atelier catalog. Orders that have advanced to PROCESSING or SHIPPED cannot be cancelled via self-service and require contact with atelier dispatch.

3. Pricing & Complimentary Courier Delivery

All prices displayed are denominated in PKR (Rs.). Standard courier transit is complimentary for domestic Pakistani orders whose subtotal exceeds Rs. 3,500. Orders beneath this threshold incur a flat Rs. 250 insured dispatch fee.

4. Promotional Privileges & Coupons

Promotional voucher codes (such as WELCOME10 or MAISON500) must be entered at checkout prior to final order confirmation. Coupons cannot be applied retroactively to completed dispatches and are subject to minimum order value thresholds.
